# Card Member Analysis & Customer Behaviour

## Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

This section explores American Express card member data to understand customer spending behaviour, transaction patterns, category preferences, and other characteristics within the dataset.

### Objectives

- Understand the structure and characteristics of the dataset
- Examine customer and transaction-level variables
- Analyze spending distributions and transaction patterns
- Identify missing values and duplicate records
- Detect and investigate potential outliers
- Explore relationships between key variables
- Identify initial patterns that can support further statistical and business analysis
